## Why this matters

Consistent posting is hard when you are busy running the business. ChatGPT can turn a short list of topics into a calendar and first drafts. The result is only good if the content is true to your business and reviewed before it goes live.

## What you’ll need

- Five to ten real topics: services, FAQs, recent projects, seasonal items, team news
- Your brand voice in a few bullets
- The platforms you actually use (for example LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
- Permission to share any customer story or photo

## Step-by-step

### 1. List your raw material

Write real material in bullet form: questions customers ask, mistakes you see, before/after stories (anonymized), behind-the-scenes moments.

### 2. Generate the calendar

```
You are a social media planner for [COMPANY], a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] serving [AUDIENCE].

Voice: [VOICE NOTES]
Platforms: [PLATFORMS]
Posting frequency: [NUMBER] posts per week
Topics and facts I can use (use only these; do not invent customer stories, statistics, or testimonials):
[PASTE TOPICS]

Create a 4-week content calendar as a table with: week, day, platform, topic, format (text, photo, short video idea), and a goal (educate, build trust, invite contact).
Then write full drafts for the first week of posts, each with a short hook and a clear call to action.
```

### 3. Add what only you have

Insert real details: your photos, local references, genuine customer feedback with permission. Remove anything generic.

### 4. Check before scheduling

Check for claims you cannot support, outdated offers, and tone. Have a second person read posts that mention pricing, guarantees, or regulated topics.

### 5. Review monthly

Look at which posts got replies or inquiries (use your platform’s own analytics) and tell ChatGPT what worked so the next calendar improves.

> **Tip:** Ask for three hook options per post and pick the one that sounds like you.

> **Watch out:** Never let a draft include invented testimonials, numbers, or before/after results.

## Tips

- Reuse one idea across platforms by asking for a version per format.
- Keep a bank of evergreen posts for busy weeks.
- Add alt text for images; ask ChatGPT to draft it, then correct it.

## Common pitfalls

- Posting AI drafts unedited, so every post sounds the same
- Making claims about results you cannot back up
- Sharing customer details without consent

## Team Safety & Privacy Note

- Get permission before featuring customers or staff.
- Do not paste confidential plans or unreleased pricing into the chat.
- Follow each platform’s rules on advertising and disclosures.
