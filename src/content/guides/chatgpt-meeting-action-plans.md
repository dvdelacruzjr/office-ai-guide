## Why this matters

Meetings fail in the last five minutes—when nobody writes who owns what. ChatGPT is excellent at structuring notes into tables and email-ready summaries your office can paste into Slack, Asana, or a CRM task.

## What you’ll need

- Raw notes or a rough transcript
- ChatGPT (Business/Enterprise recommended for internal meetings)
- Known attendees and any hard deadlines

## Step-by-step

### 1. Capture notes without formatting anxiety

Write messy bullets during the meeting. Completeness beats polish.

### 2. Convert notes with this prompt

```
Convert the meeting notes below into a structured action plan.

Meeting: [TITLE]
Date: [DATE]
Attendees: [NAMES]

Output:
1) Decisions made
2) Action items table: Task | Owner | Due date | Dependency | Status
3) Open questions
4) Risks / assumptions
5) Email summary to send to attendees (120 words)

Notes:
[PASTE NOTES]

If owner or due date is missing, mark as TBD and propose a sensible default.
```

### 3. Confirm owners live

Before sending, ping anyone marked TBD. ChatGPT can propose owners; only humans can accept them.

### 4. Store the plan where work happens

Paste the table into your project tracker. Keep the email summary for the thread of record.

## Example scenario

Illustrative scenario: an ops manager runs this after a weekly standup. Every item leaves the meeting with an owner and a date, so “someone should…” items stop floating around.

## Team Safety & Privacy Note

- Remove salary discussions, HR cases, and security incidents from notes before using consumer ChatGPT.
- Prefer ChatGPT Business/Enterprise workspace controls for internal meetings.
- Do not upload full confidential decks if a short excerpt will do.
- Human-review anything that will be forwarded to clients.
