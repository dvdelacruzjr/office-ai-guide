## Why this matters

Teams waste hours collecting “interesting” links that never become decisions. Grok Bot is useful for recurring research loops: gather, compress, and route insights to the people who sell and deliver.

## What you’ll need

- A narrow research question (not “watch the whole industry”)
- Sources your team already trusts
- A Friday delivery slot (Slack/email)

## Step-by-step

### 1. Pick one digest theme

Examples:

- Competitor packaging changes
- Local market hiring signals
- Customer objections heard this week

### 2. Run the digest prompt

```
Create a weekly research digest for our [INDUSTRY] team.

Sources / notes:
[PASTE LINKS, NOTES, OR BULLETS]

Output format:
- Top 5 takeaways for sales and ops
- What changed vs last week (assume unknown if not provided)
- Opportunities to test
- Risks to watch
- Suggested internal Slack update (120 words)

Audience: office team adopting AI, not engineers.
```

### 3. Add a recurring routine brief

```
Design a recurring calendar triage routine for a business owner who works [HOURS/WEEK] and has meetings mostly on [DAYS].

Deliver:
1) A weekly cadence (Mon–Fri) with time blocks
2) Meeting acceptance rules
3) Prep/follow-up checklist
4) What Grok Bot should remind me about each morning
5) What to decline or shorten by default

Constraints:
- Protect 2 deep-work blocks weekly
- Keep total meeting load under [HOURS] hours/week if possible
Current calendar pain points:
[DESCRIBE PAIN POINTS]
```

### 4. Close the loop with owners

End every digest with **one experiment** and a named owner. Without that, digests become newsletter wallpaper.

## Example scenario

Illustrative scenario: a B2B services team sets up a Friday digest of the objections and questions prospects raised that week, then reviews it before updating proposal language.

## Team Safety & Privacy Note

- Do not paste confidential pipeline amounts or named deal strategies into consumer sessions.
- Prefer links + short paraphrases over exporting entire CRM notes.
- Label AI-generated digests as “draft synthesis” until a human edits.
- Keep customer-identifying anecdotes anonymized.
